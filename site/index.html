DarkProof

Messages that work when the network doesn't.

DarkProof is a free messaging app for blackouts, disasters and places without signal. Phones pass messages to each other over Bluetooth, Wi-Fi and LoRa radios. No internet, no accounts, no servers.

Get DarkProof Privacy

What it does

Group chat, offline

Your family or team shares one group code. Messages are encrypted end to end, and every DarkProof phone nearby helps carry them, even phones from other groups that can't read them.

“I'm safe” in one tap

Check in as safe or ask for help. Your group sees who is fine, who needs help, their battery level and, if you choose, where you are.

Water, shelter, power

Share places where people can find water, shelter, charging or medical help. Everyone nearby sees them, sorted by distance.

Reaches further

Messages hop from phone to phone for days. Wi-Fi routers and LoRa radios (Meshtastic, MeshCore) stretch the range, and any phone that gets internet passes them on worldwide.

Built for trust

  • No sign-up. Your name and group codes stay on your phone.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. We run no servers and receive no data.
  • Your position is shared only if you turn it on, and only with your group (or with everyone, for a place you choose to share).
  • Works on iPhone and Android, and the two talk to each other.
